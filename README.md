**Neon Reign: Viretta** — an original open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser, no build step and no network calls. Play as Luca "Shade" Marquez in the neon coastal city of Viretta: five districts (Dockyard, Downtown, Casino Strip, Residential, Central Park) plus a harbor waterfront. Walk or drive anywhere, steal any parked car, outrun a 0–5 star wanted system with chasing police, roadblocks and a busted meter, take cover in gunfights, and work through a 3-mission story — *Echoes of the Harbor*, *Circuit Break* and *Neon Reckoning* — with dialogue, objectives, cash rewards and a saved Continue. Full HUD and live minimap, traffic and pedestrians, day-bright neon art drawn in code, synthesized engine/siren/combat audio. Controls: WASD/arrows to move, E enter/exit car, F or Space to shoot, Shift sprint, P/Esc pause, M mute.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
